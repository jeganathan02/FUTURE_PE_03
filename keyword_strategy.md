# SEO Keyword & Content Strategy: NEET Coaching in Chennai

## The Strategy: Hub and Spoke (Topic Cluster) Model
To rank effectively for highly competitive terms like "Best NEET coaching in Chennai," we use a topic cluster model. 
- **The Hub (Pillar Page):** A comprehensive, long-form guide covering the broad topic in depth.
- **The Spokes (Supporting Blogs):** Shorter, specific articles answering long-tail queries and "People Also Ask" questions. These link back to the Hub, passing link equity and establishing topical authority.

## 1. Pillar Blog (The Hub)
**Topic:** The Ultimate Guide to Choosing the Best NEET Coaching Center in Chennai
**Primary Keyword:** Best NEET coaching center in Chennai
**Secondary Keywords:** top medical coaching Chennai, NEET classes in Chennai, best institute for NEET in Chennai.
**Search Intent:** Commercial Investigation / Informational. Parents and students are researching and comparing different coaching institutes to make a final decision.
**Business Goal:** Establish trust, show expertise, and drive inquiries/admissions.
